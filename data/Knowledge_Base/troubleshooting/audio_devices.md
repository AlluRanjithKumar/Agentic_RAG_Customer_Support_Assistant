# PulseBuds 2 Troubleshooting Guide

**Document ID:** RT-TS-AUD-1.3  
**Effective date:** 2026-04-18

## One earbud is silent

1. Confirm that both earbuds appear in the companion application.
2. Inspect and dry-clean the charging contacts. Do not use water or sharp metal tools.
3. Charge the case and both earbuds for at least 20 minutes.
4. Check the phone's left-right audio balance and disable mono-routing overrides.
5. Reset the earbuds only if both earbuds are detected by the charging case.

If one earbud remains absent from both the case indicator and companion application, record the affected serial component and open a hardware case.

## Audio is intermittent

1. Test within one metre of the phone with other Bluetooth devices temporarily disconnected.
2. Update the companion application and earbud firmware.
3. Test with a second supported phone when one is available.
4. If the failure occurs only in one streaming application, troubleshoot that application before recommending hardware replacement.

## Charging case is hot

Stop charging immediately if the case is painful to touch, swollen, smoking or smells burnt.

When it is safe, move the case away from flammable material and mark the case as `SAFETY_ESCALATION`.

Do not request:

- Continued charging.
- A factory reset.
- An ordinary postal return.

Wait for specialist transport and handling instructions.

## Return-policy interaction

Used earbuds are not returnable for change of mind. However, the hygiene restriction does not remove repair, replacement or other remedies for an authorized, confirmed defect. Consult RT-POL-RET-2.4 before making a return decision.
