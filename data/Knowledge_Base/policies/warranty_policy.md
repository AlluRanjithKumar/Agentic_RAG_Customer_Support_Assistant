# Ranjith's Tech Limited Warranty Policy

**Document ID:** RT-POL-WAR-3.2  
**Effective date:** 2026-07-01  
**Supersedes:** RT-POL-WAR-3.1 dated 2026-01-15

## 1. General coverage

Ranjith's Tech warrants eligible hardware against manufacturing defects from the original invoice date. The duration is product-specific and is listed in `product_catalog.json`. A replacement does not restart the warranty; it carries the longer of the remaining original warranty or 90 calendar days.

Coverage includes failures of internal components under normal use, charging-port failure not caused by debris or force, and display failure without impact or liquid indicators.

## 2. Battery coverage

Rechargeable batteries are consumable components. A battery claim is eligible during the product's battery-coverage period only when diagnostic capacity is below 60% of design capacity **and** the charge-cycle count is 500 or fewer.

The cycle-count limit is waived when an authorized diagnostic records swelling, smoke, unusual heat, or enclosure separation. Safety incidents must be escalated immediately; customers must not continue charging or ship a swollen battery without instructions.

Runtime complaints alone are not proof of a battery defect. The customer must complete the approved calibration procedure unless a safety symptom is present.

## 3. Accidental damage and exclusions

The standard warranty excludes:

- Liquid ingress, impact damage, crushed connectors, fire, theft or loss.
- Cosmetic wear that does not affect operation.
- Damage caused by incompatible chargers.
- Modification or repair by a non-authorized service provider.
- Software-only problems and loss of customer data.

Customers with an active `ProtectPlus` plan receive one accidental-damage repair per 12-month plan period. A service fee applies: INR 1,499 for laptops, INR 599 for audio products, and INR 799 for wearables. ProtectPlus never covers loss, theft or intentional damage.

Opening the removable SSD access panel on an AeroBook Pro does not void coverage. Damage caused during the upgrade remains excluded.

## 4. Purchase-channel rules

Products bought directly from Ranjith's Tech or an authorized retail partner are eligible after invoice verification. Products bought from an online marketplace are eligible for technical diagnosis and warranty repair when the seller is authorized. Marketplace refunds and marketplace return-window disputes must be handled by the marketplace seller.

Second-hand products may retain transferable hardware coverage if the original invoice is supplied. ProtectPlus is not transferable.

## 5. Claim evidence

The claimant must normally provide an invoice or order ID and the device serial number. If the order exists in the internal order system, a missing paper invoice is not by itself grounds for rejection.

Photos may be requested for physical-condition review. Passwords, payment credentials and unrelated personal files must never be requested.

## 6. Resolution hierarchy

Ranjith's Tech may repair, replace with an equivalent refurbished unit, or refund when repair and replacement are unavailable. A customer cannot demand a refund solely because a repair was offered, except where applicable consumer law requires otherwise.

Three verified repairs for the same hardware fault within 90 days trigger senior review for replacement. The repairs must concern the same component; unrelated repairs do not count toward this threshold.

## 7. Precedence and ambiguity

A later-dated written policy overrides an older policy. On the same date, a product-specific rule overrides a general rule. If evidence is insufficient or documents conflict without a clear precedence rule, the agent must not guess and must escalate the case.
