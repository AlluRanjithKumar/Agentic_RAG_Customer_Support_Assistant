# Shipping, Delivery Damage and Replacement Rules

**Document ID:** RT-POL-SHIP-1.8  
**Effective date:** 2026-07-20  
**Timezone:** Asia/Kolkata

## Delivery estimates

Standard delivery normally takes 3–7 business days. Remote areas may require an additional 2–4 business days. Weather, regional restrictions and incorrect addresses can extend these estimates.

An estimated delivery date is not guaranteed unless the invoice explicitly describes it as guaranteed.

### Delayed shipments

- When tracking has no scan for 72 hours, open a carrier investigation.
- When delivery is five business days beyond the latest estimate, offer reshipment or a refund after verifying the address.
- A carrier investigation does not by itself guarantee an immediate refund.

## Delivery damage

Customers should report delivery damage within 48 hours. Preferred evidence includes:

- A photograph of the outer packaging.
- A photograph of the shipping label.
- A photograph showing the product damage.

Missing photographs do not automatically invalidate a claim when the customer cannot safely handle the item or carrier records independently confirm the damage.

If an item is hot, swollen, smoking, leaking or otherwise unsafe, instruct the customer to stop using it, keep it away from heat when safe, and await specialist instructions. Safety instructions override ordinary return-shipping instructions.

## Wrong or missing item

A wrong-item or missing-item report should be opened within seven calendar days. Compare order and serial records and review packing-weight information when available.

After verification, offer priority reshipment or a refund without a replacement or restocking fee.

## Replacement rules

### Dead on arrival

The dead-on-arrival window is seven calendar days. DOA replacement rules override change-of-mind return rules.

### Advance replacement

Advance replacement may be offered only when all of the following apply:

1. The product is a business-tier product.
2. The delivery address is serviceable.
3. Identity verification and the temporary payment hold are completed.

A temporary card hold may be placed. It is released when the original product is scanned by the return carrier within 10 days. Failure to return the original product may cause the hold to be captured after review.

### Standard replacement

An approved standard replacement should normally be dispatched within two business days. An equivalent replacement may be refurbished. It carries the warranty period defined in RT-POL-WAR-3.2 rather than starting an entirely new warranty.

### Replacement unavailable

When stock is unavailable, offer one of the following:

- Join the waitlist.
- Accept an equivalent or better model with the customer's consent.
- Receive a refund when repair and other replacement remedies are unavailable.

## Address changes

An address can be changed before dispatch after order verification. After dispatch, changes require carrier approval and are not guaranteed.

Never change both the recipient name and delivery state after dispatch without fraud-team review.

## Precedence

Safety instructions override ordinary shipping instructions. DOA rules override change-of-mind rules. If this document conflicts with a later-dated policy, use the later policy and record both sources.
