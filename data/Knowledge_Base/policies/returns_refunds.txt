RANJITH'S TECH — RETURNS AND REFUNDS
Document ID: RT-POL-RET-2.4
Effective date: 2026-08-15

STANDARD WINDOW
Direct online purchases may be returned within 30 calendar days after confirmed delivery. Authorized physical-store purchases have a 15-day window unless the invoice gives a longer period. The request must be opened before 23:59 Asia/Kolkata on the final day.

CONDITION AND CONTENTS
A non-defective return must include the main product, supplied charger or cable, and serial-number label. Missing nonessential printed material does not block a return. A reasonable deduction may be made for a missing essential accessory.

OPENED PRODUCTS
An opened, non-defective laptop or wearable carries a 10% restocking fee. Audio devices used in or on the ear cannot be returned for preference or change-of-mind hygiene reasons. An unopened audio device remains returnable during the applicable window.

The restocking fee and audio hygiene restriction do not apply when an authorized diagnosis confirms a manufacturing defect. The customer must complete the relevant troubleshooting procedure unless the reported symptom is unsafe or troubleshooting could worsen damage.

DEAD ON ARRIVAL (DOA)
A product that cannot power on or complete initial setup within 7 calendar days of delivery may qualify as DOA. After remote verification, DOA cases use the replacement process in RT-POL-SHIP-1.8. They should not be rejected merely because the packaging was opened.

LATE EXCEPTIONS
A return opened after the normal window may receive manager review when: the delivery date was recorded incorrectly; support instructed the customer in writing to wait; or a verified service outage prevented submission on the final day. Manager review is not automatic approval.

REFUND TIMING
Inspection normally takes 2–4 business days after receipt. Approved refunds are initiated to the original payment method within 2 business days. Banks may require a further 5–10 business days. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded to a verified Indian bank account or UPI ID; staff must not request an OTP or PIN.

CANCELLATIONS
An order can be cancelled without charge before warehouse dispatch. After dispatch, it follows the return process. Refusing delivery is permitted, but outbound expedited-shipping fees are not refundable unless the wrong item was shipped.

MARKETPLACE PURCHASES
Marketplace refunds are governed and issued by the marketplace seller. Ranjith's Tech can diagnose the product and provide a service report but must not promise that a marketplace refund will be approved.

PRECEDENCE
Product-specific promotional return terms shown on the original invoice override this general document when they provide the customer a longer window. A shorter promotional term does not reduce statutory rights.
